⚠️ Non-Negotiable Rules for Sedentary Occupations
⏱️
De-Sit Every 50 Mins
Forced reminders. Move for 120 seconds. This disrupts sedentary patterns and mitigates stubborn fat adaptations.
🚫
Eliminate Modern Sugars
Processed snacks trigger dramatic glycemic drops, signaling visceral fat storage right at your midsection. Keep it completely out.
🌙
Pre-8 PM Depletion Lock
Metabolic processing naturally slows overnight. Early, light dining aligns caloric load with active circadian rhythms.
😴
7.5 Hour Cellular Sleep
Rest gaps elevate resting cortisol levels, which hoards fat. Consider proper deep sleep as a strict metabolic requirement.
🥚
Defend 140g Daily Protein
Protects existing structural muscle from breakdown during deficits. Miss your macro goals, and your body depletes lean tissue instead of fat.
📊
Monday Fasted Metrics
Track weight immediately upon waking. Supplement this with navel measurements—waist metrics always tell the true shred timeline.
