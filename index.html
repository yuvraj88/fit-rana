⚠️ Non-Negotiable Rules for a Desk Job Body
⏱️
Stand Every 45–60 Min
Set a timer. Even 2-minute walks reduce cortisol — the belly fat hormone. Non-negotiable.
🚫
Zero Refined Sugar & Maida
These spike insulin and promote visceral fat storage. Biscuits, white bread, packaged snacks — all out.
🌙
Dinner Before 8 PM
Late eating directly impacts belly fat at 37. Your metabolism slows after 8 PM. Eat light, eat early.
😴
7–8 Hours Sleep
Poor sleep = elevated cortisol = more belly fat stored. Sleep is literally part of your fat-loss plan.
🥚
Hit 140g+ Protein Daily
At 37, muscle preservation requires consistent protein. Skip it and your body burns muscle, not fat.
📊
Weigh Every Monday Morning
Fasted, same time, same day. Also track waist measurement at navel — it's more revealing than scale weight.
